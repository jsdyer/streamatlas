Station metadata, not a live guarantee

Radio Browser is a community-maintained directory. Its last check, location, genre and bitrate can be incomplete or out of date. The retained shelf is a dated starting point; directory search reaches beyond it. A listed station can be offline or unavailable in your region.

Press play when you’re ready

No audio starts automatically. Play connects directly to a station’s HTTPS stream and reports a station click to Radio Browser. HTTP-only streams open outside this page. Some formats, HLS playlists or geographic restrictions may require the station’s own player. No audio is recorded or proxied here.

Privacy & sources

Search words go to Radio Browser. Playing reveals your IP address to the station and its stream host. Your station collection is not saved between visits unless you opt in. Radio Browser publishes its directory metadata as public domain; the stations retain rights in their broadcasts. API documentation.

CoolCode’s shared visit counter may record aggregate visits. The application does not send your collection to CoolCode. You can expand the country suggestions, quick searches and retained shelf in the repository’s editable config.
